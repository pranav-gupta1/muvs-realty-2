# MUVS Realty website: details we need

The new MUVS Realty website is built and working. To launch it, we need the details below.
Anything you are not sure about, leave blank and add a note. Please send documents and photos
as separate files (PDF, JPG or PNG).

---

## 1. Contact details

These appear on every page. Visitors cannot reach you until they are filled in.

- [ ] **Office phone number.** Example: +91 98102 02834. Shown in the header, footer and contact page.
- [ ] **WhatsApp number.** With country code and no spaces, for example +919810202834. Used for the chat button on every page. Can be the same as the phone number.
- [ ] **Public email address.** For example info@muvsrealty.com. Shown on the site and used for replies to enquiries.
- [ ] **Office address.** Full address including sector, city and PIN code.
- [ ] **Google Maps link to the office.** Open the office in Google Maps, tap Share, and send the link.
- [ ] **Office hours.** The site currently says "Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM". Please confirm or correct.

## 2. RERA registration

- [ ] **UP RERA agent registration number.** It usually looks like UPRERAAGT12345. By law this has to appear on the website, and it will be shown at the bottom of every page.
- [ ] **A scan or clear photo of the RERA certificate.**

## 3. About the director

- [ ] **Full name**, exactly as it should appear.
- [ ] **Designation.** Currently "Director, MUVS Realty". Please confirm.
- [ ] **A short bio, 2 to 3 paragraphs.** How you got into real estate, the areas you know best, notable deals or developers you have worked with, and what clients can expect when they work with you.
- [ ] **A one or two line quote** in the director's own words about how they work with clients. Optional.
- [ ] **Credentials.** Degrees, professional memberships, years in the business. A simple list is fine.
- [ ] **A professional photo.** Portrait orientation, at least 1200 pixels tall, with a plain or office background.

## 4. Social media

Send only the ones you use. Anything left out is simply not shown.

- [ ] Instagram profile link
- [ ] Facebook page link
- [ ] LinkedIn link (company page or the director's profile)
- [ ] YouTube channel link

## 5. Company numbers

These count up on the home page. The current figures are placeholders. Please send the real ones.

- [ ] **Years in business** (placeholder: 12)
- [ ] **Properties sold or leased** (placeholder: 480)
- [ ] **Cities you work in** (placeholder: 6)
- [ ] **Clients served** (placeholder: 1,200)

## 6. Please confirm or remove

The site currently lists these as sample content. We will remove anything that is not confirmed,
because they cannot be shown unless they are true.

- [ ] **Membership of NAR India (National Association of Realtors, India), 2022.** Do you hold this? If yes, please send the certificate.
- [ ] **ISO 9001:2015 certification, 2024.** Do you hold this? If yes, send the certificate and the name of the certifying body.
- [ ] **Empanelled channel partner, 2021.** Which developers have empanelled you? Please send the empanelment letters.
- [ ] **Any other certificates or awards** you would like shown.

## 7. Photos, videos and client reviews

- [ ] **A main banner photo for the home page.** Landscape, at least 2400 pixels wide. A project you have sold, or a good view of the area.
- [ ] **YouTube links to your own videos**: property tours, site visits, client reviews or market updates. The site currently shows sample videos from other channels.
- [ ] **3 to 5 client testimonials.** For each: the client's name, what they bought and where, a few lines in their words, and their permission to publish it.

## 8. Your actual listings

Everything currently on the site (7 projects and 18 resale listings) is sample data and will be
replaced.

- [ ] **Projects you are selling.** For each: project name, developer, location and sector, price range, unit sizes, possession date, RERA project number, the brochure (PDF), photos and floor plans.
- [ ] **Resale properties.** For each: type (flat, plot, villa, land, shop, office, shed), location, price, area, the key details, and photos.

## 9. For whoever sets up the website

These are only needed when the site goes live.

- [ ] **SMS for phone verification.** An MSG91 account (recommended for India) with the auth key, a DLT-approved OTP template ID and the sender ID. For international numbers, a Twilio account instead.
- [ ] **Email sending.** SMTP details from Google Workspace or Zoho Mail (server, username and an app password), plus the inbox that should receive new enquiries.
- [ ] **Domain and hosting access.** Login for the muvsrealty.com domain settings (DNS) and the hosting account, for example Vercel.
